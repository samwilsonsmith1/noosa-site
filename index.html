What it does

What visitors ask a local, in one calm app: what's good right now, what's on, where to go, and a plan for your days here.

Ideas for right now

Tap through a few ideas that suit this moment, from the ferry up the river to dinner on the water.

What's on, today and this week

Live music, markets, trivia and Noosa's big festivals, checked with the venues every week.

Places worth your time

Beaches, national park walks, cafes, restaurants, tours and hinterland days, each with the best time to go.

The best of Noosa on one map, with walking tracks drawn on, and a plan for each day of your trip.
