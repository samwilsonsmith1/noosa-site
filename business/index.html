How it works

1

Send us your details

A new listing or a fix to an old one: your hours, what you do, your website.

2

We check it

We check it against your own site and write it up in the app's voice. A local approves every one before it goes in.

3

You're in

Visitors find you on the map, in Explore, in ideas for a day, and can add you to their plan.

A few house rules

  • Photos you send must be your own, or ones you're allowed to share.
  • Food deals and events are welcome. We never list happy hours, drink deals or drink prices (Queensland liquor law).
  • We write what's true from your own site, and keep it current. Tell us when something changes.

Cards for your front desk

Hotels, cafes and shops can hand guests a small card with a QR code to the app. Free, and we'll drop them in.
